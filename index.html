About Me

I'm a Senior Embedded Systems Engineer based in Santa Clara, California, with 12+ years of experience in system software development, embedded platforms, and low-level hardware integration. My career spans aerospace (Airbus), networking (Cisco), consumer electronics and automotive (Google), and defense (DRDO).

I specialize in Embedded C/C++ for Linux-based systems including kernel modules, device drivers, and board support package development. I build libraries and abstraction layers that translate high-level APIs into low-level OS and hardware driver operations. I also have strong knowledge of GPU systems and graphics programming using the Vulkan API.

Beyond my professional work, I'm the founder of EoS (Embedded Operating System) — an open-source embedded OS platform with a full ecosystem including a build system, simulator, browser, AI layer, office suite, and more.
